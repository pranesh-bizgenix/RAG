About this Copilot

The BIZGENIX AI Knowledge Copilot answers questions using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): it searches the documents in the knowledge base, then writes an answer from the passages it found and cites them as [1], [2], and so on.

Click a citation or a source chip to see the exact passage. If the documents don't cover a question, the Copilot says so.

Documents are added and removed in the Admin Panel.
